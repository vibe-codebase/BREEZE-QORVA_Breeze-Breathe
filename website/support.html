01FAQ

Good to know

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in Breeze Breathe and tap Reset everything. That clears what the app keeps on this phone: your breathing sessions, journal, chosen pattern and sound mix. Uninstalling Breeze Breathe or clearing its storage also removes everything.

Does Breeze Breathe need the internet?

No. Breeze Breathe does not request the Internet permission, so it works anywhere, including in flight mode, and it never sends anything off your phone.

Is it free? Are there ads?

It is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create.

Where is my data stored?

Only in the app's private storage on your phone. Nobody else, including us, can see it. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your personal backup, under your control.

Why does it use Vibrate?

For optional haptic feedback at each breathing phase, so you can follow with your eyes closed. You can switch it off in Settings. It gives the app no access to any information.

What is Breathe in Breeze Breathe?

The phase and seconds sit inside the orb, with a ring for the whole session. Pick 1 to 15 minutes; sessions round to whole breaths. Pause, resume or finish early.

What is Patterns in Breeze Breathe?

Coherent (5.5 · 5.5), Box (4 · 4 · 4 · 4), 4 · 7 · 8, Long exhale and Wake up, each with a timing bar, plus your own pattern from four steppers.

What is Sounds in Breeze Breathe?

Ocean, rain and brown-noise faders, made on the phone, with presets and a preview. They play during your sessions.

Is it medical or professional advice?

Breeze Breathe is a relaxation and mindfulness aid. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Which devices does it run on?

Breeze Breathe runs on Android 7.0 or newer, on phones and tablets.
