01Features

Made to do the job

One glowing orb fills as you breathe in, waits while you hold and empties as you breathe out.

01

Breathe

The phase and seconds sit inside the orb, with a ring for the whole session. Pick 1 to 15 minutes; sessions round to whole breaths. Pause, resume or finish early.

02

Patterns

Coherent (5.5 · 5.5), Box (4 · 4 · 4 · 4), 4 · 7 · 8, Long exhale and Wake up, each with a timing bar, plus your own pattern from four steppers.

03

Sounds

Ocean, rain and brown-noise faders, made on the phone, with presets and a preview. They play during your sessions.

04

Journal

Your streak, minutes this week, a seven-day chart and every session.

Made for calm

  • A soft bell between phases and a vibration at each phase, so you can close your eyes.
  • Keeps the screen on during a session if you want.
  • Dusk and Dawn looks.

Please noteBreeze Breathe is a relaxation and mindfulness aid. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

03Trust

Breeze Breathe works entirely on your phone. Private by design.

  • Works offlineBreeze Breathe has no Internet permission, so nothing can leave your phone.
  • No accountOpen it and start. No sign-up, no email address, no password.
  • No ads, no trackingNo adverts, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No data collectedWhat you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • One permissionOnly Vibrate, for optional haptics at each breathing phase, so you can follow with your eyes closed. Switch it off in Settings.
