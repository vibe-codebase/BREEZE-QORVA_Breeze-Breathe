Privacy Policy

Nothing about you leaves your phone. The full policy follows.

Breeze Breathe by BREEZE QORVA · Effective 22 November 2026

Breeze Breathe (com.breezeqorva.breezebreathe) is an everyday tool app published by BREEZE QORVA. It runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

01

Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device information. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Personal information such as name, email address or phone number.
  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID.
  • Location, precise or approximate.
  • Contacts, photos, videos, audio or files.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics.

There are no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

02

No internet access

The release app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over any network. All art, sound and fonts are built into the app.

03

What stays on your device

What you enter (your breathing sessions, journal, chosen pattern and sound mix) and your settings are kept only in the app's private storage on your device and are never transmitted. Nobody else, including us, can see them. Reset everything in Settings, uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include them in your personal backup, under your control.

04

Not medical or professional advice

Breathing exercises are for relaxation. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. If you feel dizzy or unwell, stop and breathe normally.

05

Permissions

The only permission is Vibrate, used for optional haptic feedback at each breathing phase, so you can follow with your eyes closed. You can switch it off in the app's Settings. It gives the app no access to any information.

06

Children

The app is a general tool for people aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

07

Your rights

As we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything you enter in the app is fully under your control.

08

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
